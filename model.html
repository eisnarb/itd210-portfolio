The Synthesizer

The synthesizer is a foundational instrument in electronic music production. It works both as a sound source, being able to create raw sound using electrical signals, and creative tool that manipulates sounds.

The modular synthesizer is the most used type of synthesizer for modern music production and you can scroll below to explore a model of the Moog Grandmother synth and learn more about its parts.

Patchbay A hub that allows users to control the routing of audio inputs/outputs using patch cables.

Arpeggiator / Sequencer The arpeggiator converts chords into a repeating pattern of the individual chord's notes. The sequencer plays back a programmed series of notes determined by the user.

Spring Reverb This tool is used to add reverb, or sound reflection, onto the sound being created. Typically adds a bright, metallic effect onto the sound.
